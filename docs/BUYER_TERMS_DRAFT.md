# Buyer terms for owner review — NOT A LIVE POLICY

**Status: PROPOSED.** These are candidate plain-language terms. They have **not** been approved by the seller, published as binding checkout terms, or verified against the merchant's rules and applicable law. Do not present them as already effective.

## Proposed license

A single purchaser may use and adapt the **paid** Markdown starter files and templates for their own projects and projects they build for clients. They may not redistribute, resell, or publish the paid ZIP or standalone paid templates as a competing download. This does not restrict access to the public core instructions already available on GitHub or rights granted by other applicable licenses.

## Proposed refund policy — seller choice required

**Candidate:** 14-day refund request window after purchase for a buyer who finds the digital bundle unsuitable, including a failure of the advertised files to deliver. A no-questions-asked policy may reduce friction but needs the seller's explicit approval and must fit the chosen merchant's capabilities and applicable law.

Do not add this proposal to a checkout page until the seller confirms the window, exclusions if any, contact method, who processes the refund, and what the merchant actually supports. Nothing here reduces mandatory consumer rights.

## Proposed support boundary

The purchase includes the exact versioned Markdown ZIP and delivery/access support via the merchant's chosen contact method. It does **not** include custom implementation, agent installation, code repair, a security review, 1:1 troubleshooting, or guaranteed future upgrades.

## Proposed update policy

A purchase includes the version identified in the store listing. The seller may publish revisions, but ongoing updates are not guaranteed unless the listing expressly includes them. Public core improvements remain accessible in the GitHub repository.

## Claim discipline

This is reusable written guidance, not software that enforces agent behavior. Buyer remains responsible for reviewing, testing and approving their app. Do not promise code quality, safety, regulatory compliance, or that a third-party model will follow instructions. Buyer communications and policies must match the checkout page and the delivered ZIP.
